# The Port (D3, with B3's boards): names she hears, not reads

For Zosia at 3.5. She toots a boat's name on the horn, one toot per piece (syllable). Level 3 asked "Która łódka ma dwa
kawałki?" over three written name boards and never said the names, so it needed reading. The boards were as wide as
the name was long, so the widest board was always "three pieces".

## Why pictures

One width alone leaves the written name's length as the cue: KOT is short, BIEDRONKA is long, and the longest word is
nearly always "three pieces". A picture carries no length. It also lets her keep each boat's name in mind: she hears
"Foka!" and sees the seal. The syllable blocks still appear when the name is clapped or tooted back, and every
board is the same width (room for three blocks).

## Taps during the names

A tick tapped while the names are still being said is not an answer yet (she has not heard what is asked): it gets
the usual tick and bounce. Any tap of hers stops the names; a tap on a boat says that name, then the question.

## A wrong tap at level 3

The ladder: "Ta łódka ma trzy kawałki." and the question again; a second wrong boat sails off; then the right board
pulses. The clue (a long wait) is the names again, each boat hopping, not the answer.

## Not piling up the name

Level 3 says each name once before the question and the right boat's name once after. A tap on a boat says one name,
when she asks for it. Levels 1 and 2 keep B4's once before and once after.
