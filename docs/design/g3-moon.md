# G3 Moon Jumps (plan units B3 and D9)

## What she sees and does
Chase lands in his helmet. In a `jump` round she taps Chase once per jump and every landing is counted. In a `howMany` round Chase jumps by himself, she may tap each footprint pair to count, then picks the numeral.

From today:
- Footprints are big: each pair is up to 1.44 world units wide, about 80 px of plane and 65 px of print on the iPad (they were 49 px of plane). The row shares a fixed 11.6 units, so a pair is as wide as 1.44 where the round leaves few of them and narrows only for a long row (7 jumps and her extras: about 50 px).
- Chase is drawn 1.4 times as big (about 120 px wide), the camera sits lower so he clears the speech bubble, and his own floaty jumps are a little lower (1.8) so they stay under it.
- The flag stands up after her first jump. Before it, the question is only "Skocz trzy razy! Dotknij Czejsa, to skoczy!". After the first landing: "Jeden! Dotknij flagi, kiedy skończysz!".
- Levels 1 and 2 have four rounds: howMany, jump, howMany, jump (the last jump is automatic). That is two scored questions, not one.
- A wrong flag climbs the help ladder instead of going silent: miss 1, "Za mało! Skacz dalej!" and the count asked again. Miss 2, the footprint outlines. Miss 3, Chase (or the flag, once there are enough footprints) pulses and "Tutaj!". "Too many" says "Za dużo! Teraz dobrze?" and then asks for the flag.
- The long-wait clue no longer says "tap Chase" when she already has the right number: it says the flag.
