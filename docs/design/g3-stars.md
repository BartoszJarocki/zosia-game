## What she sees and does

The first star already shines. Chase asks "Jeden, dwa, a co dalej?" and she taps the next number. A gold line draws, the number is said, the next question opens at once.

The speech bubble used to show "2 ⭐", the answer. It now shows what she has so far and a question mark, the last three numbers at most, with the arrow the River's bubble uses:

```

 +---------------+
            *3          <- the stars she may tap, each with its numeral tile
   *1 ----- *2
   [1]      [2]   [3]
```

The picture sits 0.5 units right of the view's middle, so the wider bubble stays off the picture's top star.

The bubble is hidden while the finished picture fills with colour and Chase names it.
