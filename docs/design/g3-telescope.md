# G3 The Telescope (plan unit D10)

## What she sees and does
Levels 1 to 5 are as before. The order level (6) used to ask "Co jest po Marsie?" with nothing to look at: the order was only in her memory. It now draws the road from the Sun along the top of the eyepiece, the Sun and the eight planets in order, small, with one place empty:

```
   ( brass eyepiece )
   [Sun] [Mercury] [Venus] [?] [Mars] [Jupiter] [Saturn] [Uranus] [Neptune]     <- the road
                                                                                  "Co jest po Wenus?"
        ( Earth? )              ( Saturn )

                     ( Mars )                                                   <- the three choices
```

She taps the planet that goes in the gap. The right one flies into the "?" and the road is read as before ("Tak! To jest Ziemia."). A wrong tap says no and asks again; the second miss takes one wrong choice away; the third makes the right one pulse. The clue (after a pause or the second miss on a two-choice level) walks her eye along the road from the Sun to the "?", one place at a time, without naming one.
A place on the road says its name when tapped ("To jest Wenus.") and is never a wrong answer. The "?" asks the question again.

## What changes from today
- Order rounds draw the road; the choices move 0.6 lower to make room.
- The road makes the answer findable by looking (the planet missing from the road). That is the support the plan asks for ("the road from the Sun with one place missing"): the road is how the order is learnt by seeing it round after round, not a trick.
- A gentler run: one choice fewer; order and feature fall back to names; names with two choices to matching; matching with two choices is a round shorter (levels 1 and 2 used to equal their gentler run).
- No new voice line: the names are the first lines of the space facts.
