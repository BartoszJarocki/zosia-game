# The Reef (D2, with B3's random order): one scene, seen from the side

For Zosia at 3.5. She learns na, pod, w and obok (on, under, in, next to). Before, three little scenes stood in one row
seen from above and ran into each other, "pod kamieniem" was drawn in front of the rock, "na muszli" and "w muszli" put
the animal in the same spot, the top level's glowing spots could hardly be seen, and the two-word level went na, pod,
na, pod.

## What she sees

One scene under water, seen from the side, as if she were diving: blue water, sand at the bottom, sea grass. On the
left a big grey rock with a ledge; under the ledge is a dark hollow. On the right a big pink shell, open, its lid up.

| Place | Where the animal is |
|---|---|
| na kamieniu | on top of the rock |
| pod kamieniem | in the hollow under the ledge, only its eyes showing in the dark |
| obok kamienia | on the sand left of the rock |
| w muszli | inside the open shell, peeking out |
| obok muszli | on the sand right of the shell |

"Na" is only on the rock and "w" only in the shell, so the two never share a spot. "Na muszli" is no longer asked.

## What she does, per level

| Level | Flow | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 `see` (na, pod) | Two crabs (say) in the one scene, one on the rock, one under it. "Który krab jest pod kamieniem?" The bubble shows a little picture of the place. She taps a crab. | One scene with two animals instead of two scenes. |
| 2 `hear` (na, pod, w) | Three animals of one kind in three places, words only. | The same. |
| 3 `hear` (+ obok) | The same with four words. | The same. |
| 4 `place` | The animal swims above the scene. Three large bright rings mark places. "Połóż kraba pod kamieniem." She taps a ring and it swims there. | The rings are large, yellow and pulse, in the side view. |

The other animals (or rings) make the question about the place word: with na and pod both on the rock, the rock
alone does not tell which. Where a word has a partner on the same thing (na and pod; w and obok muszli), the scene
uses it. The rings at level 4 follow the same rule; every place is far enough from every other to tap, so the old
"spread the spots apart" rule (which kept na and pod apart, and so let "kamieniu" alone answer) is gone.

## The order of words

Each round picks a word at random, never the same one three times in a row (GAME.md already says so). Level 1 no longer
alternates na, pod, na, pod.

## Help

The ladder: "Nie, to nie ten." and the question again; then one wrong animal swims away (level 1, with two: the animals
hop one by one, "Popatrz uważnie!"); then the right one pulses. A long wait at level 4 gives "Popatrz na wszystkie
miejsca." with the rings pulsing one by one.

## Voice

No new line. The start drops "Zaglądamy przez szklaną podłogę łódki!", which no longer matches a side view.
"Popatrz na wszystkie obrazki." is no longer said (there are no pictures to compare).
