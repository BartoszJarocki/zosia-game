# G4 · Venus's Clouds

Goal for Zosia: she can see the shape behind the clouds, and nobody says the clouds are opening when they are not.

## What she sees and does, per level

Venus is a round planet with a pale window in it. Two cloud banks cover the window. A shape sits behind them. She
picks the same shape from the tiles below.

```
        [ bubble: ? ☁ ]                    ( Chase's window )
              ___________
            /   cloud     \       the window is pale cream; the shape in it is deep blue
           |  [ shape ]    |      (levels 1-2: the clouds slide apart on their own;
            \  cloud      /        levels 3-4: only a corner or a curve peeks out until she answers)
              -----------
        [ tile ]  [ tile ]  [ tile ]     the same deep blue on cream plates
```

| Level | clouds | shape | tiles |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | open on their own | koło, kwadrat, trójkąt as drawn in a textbook | 2 |
| 2 | open on their own | the same | 3 |
| 3 | stay shut but for a peek | the same | 3 |
| 4 | stay shut but for a peek | turned and resized like the Garage's | 3 |

Help: a wrong tile shakes, "Nie, to nie ten." and the question again, nothing more. On levels 3 and 4 the first clue
opens the clouds ("Chmury się rozsuwają! Jaki to kształt?"); on the open levels it says what the shape is like ("Koło
jest okrągłe!"). Then one wrong tile goes away, then the right tile pulses.

## What changes from today

1. **Contrast.** The shape and the tiles were yellow (`#facc15`, with an amber glow) on a yellow window (`#f7cf72`):
   in the review's contact sheet the shape cannot be told from the window. Now the window is pale cream (`#ffeab0`)
   and the shape and all tiles are deep blue (`#1e40af`), about 7 to 1 against the window and the cream plates (a test
   asserts at least 4.5 to 1). All shapes share one colour, so colour never says which shape it is.
2. **The words.** The question was "Chmury się rozsuwają. Jaki to kształt?" every time: said when the clouds were
   already open, and when they stayed shut (review P12). Now the question is "Jaki to kształt?". "Chmury się
   rozsuwają!" is said only when a clue really opens them.
3. **A tap while the clouds slide apart counts.** It was dropped for the first 0.85 s of each open level (review P8).
4. Voice: two new clips ("Jaki to kształt?", "Chmury się rozsuwają!"). The old two-sentence clip is no longer used.
