# D5 Saturn: sorting by two things

Today a piece floats in and she drags it to the ice ring or the rock ring. The bubble shows ❄️ or 🪨, so no level asks
anything. A drop is measured on a flat plane below the tilted rings, so a drop on the front of a ring can land in the
wrong ring or nowhere, and a tap on the planet falls through to a ring and answers for her.

After D5 the lower levels sort by kind and the top levels sort by two things at once, kind and size.

## What she sees and does

```
   bubble [ ? 🪐 ]                              (Chase)
                      .-~~~~~~~~~~~~~-.
                 .-~~   (  Saturn  )   ~~-.      inner ring: ice (blue, a crystal on it)
                (   ice ring ...........   )     outer ring: rock (brown, a stone on it)
                 '-.___[BIG]____[sm]___.-'       top levels: each ring has a big hole on the
                                                 left and a small hole on the right
                         [ piece ]               <- one piece at a time, big or small
```

- **Holes**: each is a deeper violet than the sky with a white edge, so it reads as a slot to fill, not a missing
  piece of ring. The piece it takes fills a share of it.
- **Kind levels**: one hole at the front of each ring. She drags the piece to its ring or taps the ring.
- **Both levels**: each ring has a big hole (left half) and a small hole (right half). A big ice crystal goes to the
  big hole of the ice ring. Tapping anywhere on the half ring that holds a hole picks that hole.
- **Where it lands is where it is drawn**: a drop counts on the ring or half ring drawn under the piece, found with a
  ray from the camera through the piece. The planet is tappable and answers nothing (a tick and a little bounce).
- **Bubble**: shows the kind only when Chase names it (level 1). Otherwise a question mark.
- **Help**: the clue names the piece ("To jest lód." / "To duży kawałek lodu."), the third miss pulses the right ring
  or half ring. When the winding down cuts the run, the holes close before "Pierścienie Saturna są naprawione!".
