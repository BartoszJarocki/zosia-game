# D7 The Black Hole: taking away

Today the Black Hole is the Moon's game in space. She sends pebbles (counting), and in the other rounds she watches
pebbles fall and says how many fell. Levels 1 and 2 ask one scored question a visit. A wrong count on the button gets
the scene's own "Za mało!" or "Za dużo!", so the help never climbs to the answer, and "too many" trims her beads to
the right number, which answers for her.

After D7 the watching rounds become taking away: "Trzy kamyki! Jeden wpadł. Ile zostało?"

## What she sees and does

```
   bubble [ ? ✨ ]                    (Chase)
                    ( black hole )

          [ 1 ]      [ 2 ]      [ 3 ]        <- numeral tiles, after the fall
   +--------------------------------------+
   |    (●)   (●)   (○)                   |  <- the shelf: 3 pebbles, one fell (an outline stays)
   +--------------------------------------+
```

- **Take away (`away`)**. The shelf fills with N pebbles in a row and Chase says how many ("Trzy kamyki!", the drop
  round's own clip). The last K pebbles lift off, 0.4 s apart, and spiral into the black hole. Each one leaves a pale
  outline where it lay. Chase: "Jeden wpadł." Then three numeral tiles come up and "Ile zostało?". She can tap each
  pebble left on the shelf to count it ("Jeden! Dwa!"), then taps a tile. Right: "Tak! Dwa!" and the pebbles left
  glow. The question is open from the start of the round. Its skill is `quantity`, its item the number left.
- **Drop (`drop`)**, as before: "Wypuść trzy kamyki!" and she taps or drags pebbles, each lighting a bead and counted
  aloud. The button appears after her first pebble, so it is never a trap before she starts.
- **The ladder on the button.** A wrong count is a wrong answer on the shared ladder: "Za mało! Wypuść jeszcze!" or,
  for too many, "Za dużo! Policz jeszcze raz!" with all beads going dark, then the question again. The second miss
  shows the bead outlines (the clue). The third pulses the pebble or the button (the answer).
- **Help in `away`**: the clue lights the pebbles left one at a time ("Policzmy razem!", no number said), the second
  miss takes a wrong tile away, the third pulses the right tile.
