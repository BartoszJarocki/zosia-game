## How to read this

One box is one unit of work. Every box names the evidence that checks it. A nested box is a sub-step of the box above it. Check a box only when its evidence exists, a file, a log line, a screenshot, a test run, or a SHA. The body is a how-to. The appendices explain and record.

The program runs `pstack/skills/poteto-mode/playbooks/autopilot-full.md`, adapted to this repo the way the November program ran. A unit is a branch `fx<n>` in its own worktree under `.claude/worktrees/`. The word PR below means that branch. The coordinator merges each verified unit into local `main` with `--no-ff` and pushes it. Production deploys stay the parent's (`vercel deploy --prod`). Units FX2 to FX8 change something she sees or hears, so they wait for the operator's look before merge.

A live lane is one scripted run of the repo's child-like player on the real GPU at `?q=high` (`tools/playtest/kid.mjs`, `playcheck.mjs`, `probes.mjs`, `parent.mjs`). One verifier on `sonnet` runs all ten lanes of a unit and reads their sheets, rather than ten agents. This keeps the token cost the parent asked for. Play output goes to the worktree's `tools/playtest/playcheck-out/`, never to `/tmp`, with at most two browsers at a time.

Tests alone are not sufficient verification. A PR is verified only when its unit, live, and perf boxes are all checked.
